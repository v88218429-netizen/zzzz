# Inventory and supply

1. Use live stock plus a recent demand window; never estimate stock from orders alone.
2. Calculate days of cover using an explicit demand basis and label that basis.
3. Distinguish FBS seller stock from WB warehouse stock and geographic availability.
4. Before recommending ad scaling, check stock cover and confirmed replenishment.
5. Do not assume a planned shipment has arrived until the WB status confirms it.
6. Flag zero-stock/near-stockout, stranded inventory, overstock and warehouse concentration separately.
7. For a supply recommendation, show the demand window, safety buffer and target days of cover rather than only a quantity.
8. Never modify FBS stock records automatically without tenant-specific authorization and reconciliation controls.
